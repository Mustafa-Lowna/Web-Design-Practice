The Peacock

A Vibrant Forest Bird

🦚 The peacock, with its shimmering blue and green feathers, is a symbol of beauty and pride in India. Found in forests and open lands, it is known for its graceful dance and vibrant tail display during courtship. Peacocks roost in tall trees at night to stay safe from predators.

Interesting Facts

  • Peacocks are famous for their colorful tail feathers.
  • Only males are called peacocks; females are peahens.
  • Peacocks can fly short distances.
  • They can live up to 20 years in the wild.
  • In India, the peacock is the national bird.
