Saudi Arabia, KSA

Saudi Arabia is a vast and influential country located in the heart of the Arabian Peninsula. It is known for its rich Islamic heritage, being home to the two holiest cities in Islam—Mecca and Medina which attract millions of pilgrims each year. The nation is a major global oil exporter, with petroleum playing a central role in its economy and international relations. Governed by a monarchy, Saudi Arabia is currently undergoing significant social and economic reforms under its Vision 2030 plan, aiming to modernize the country and reduce its dependence on oil. With its blend of ancient traditions and ambitious modernization, Saudi Arabia stands at a unique crossroads of history and progress.

Dubai, United Arab Emirates

Dubai is the most populous city in the United Arab Emirates and the capital of the Emirate of Dubai. Once a small fishing village, it has transformed into a global metropolis known for its futuristic skyline, luxury shopping, and world-class attractions like the Burj Khalifa and Palm Jumeirah. Despite having limited oil reserves, Dubai has built a diversified economy based on trade, tourism, aviation, and finance. The city is home to over 200 nationalities, making it one of the most cosmopolitan places in the world. With its blend of tradition and innovation, Dubai continues to be a symbol of ambition and rapid development in the Middle East.
